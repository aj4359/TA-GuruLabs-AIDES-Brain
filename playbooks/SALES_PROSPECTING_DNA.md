# Sales and Prospecting DNA | TA GuruLabs and JingWu Media
Version 1.0 · 13 November 2026

Apply this guidance using the [portfolio sales cards](PORTFOLIO_SALES_APPLICATION.md), including the first-sale experiment and product-specific gates.

## The operating rule
Show something useful, let people come to us, ask about real behaviour, then offer one appropriate next step. Prospecting here means learning where a problem or audience interest already exists in public discussions and inbound feedback. Do not collect contact lists, send unsolicited emails, DMs or connection requests, or run automatic follow-ups. Silence is missing evidence. Likes, praise and views are not intent.

## The AIDE handoff
1. Scout reads an accessible source and records title, URL, date or undated, what was actually reviewed, claim, counterexample and applicability. Use notes in our own words; no copied scripts, media, paid chapters or unavailable transcripts.
2. Sales Genius translates one idea into a question or experiment. Identify brand, audience, real problem, evidence asset, exact CTA, success signal and what would disprove the idea. Navigator classifies actual inbound and captures the last example, present workaround and requested next step, with minimum necessary data.
3. Compliance checks permissions, evidence stage, rights and scope. AUREUS Finance checks economics only for sale-ready offers. Public Learning Desk owns release review, meaningful original/licensed media and tested destinations. Record results in the existing desk; no new schedule, messages, publication or pricing changes are authorised by this playbook.
4. In each existing review cycle, select at most one newly supported idea worth testing. Compare substantive replies, repeated problems, evidence requests, qualified briefs, checkout completions and verified revenue. Record observation, interpretation and decision (keep, change, drop). Small samples and non-responses cannot establish causation.

## Evidence notes and decisions
- [SalesBendagra channel](https://www.youtube.com/@salesbendagra): supplied Short screenshot shows an AI label and a categorical headline against round prices. Channel retrieval did not expose the clip or transcript. Treat the claim as a hypothesis, not a transcript or verified demonstration.
- The supplied cover of Bendagra’s *The Closer’s Playbook* identifies discovery, negotiation, objections and ethical closing as topics. Full book not reviewed. Use these as research themes only. No attribution of specific techniques to unread chapters.
- [Itani, Goad and Jaramillo (2019)](https://scholarworks.utrgv.edu/marketing_fac/98/) synthesises sales listening research. Adopt: ask about the last actual instance, listen, reflect back and confirm. Do not claim this guarantees conversion.
- [Mason et al. (2013)](https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/precise-offers-are-potent-anchors-conciliatory-counteroffers-and-attributions) found stronger anchoring from precise first offers in negotiations. [Lee et al. (2018), negotiation entry](https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/too-precise-pursue-how-precise-offers-discourage-negotiation-entry) provides the counterweight: precision can deter entry. Reject “never use round prices”. Use clear, approved fixed prices for genuinely sale-ready self-service offers; use detailed quotes only when backed by real scope and costs. No invented precision, false scarcity or changes based on the clip.
- Objections: distinguish confusion, bad fit, missing evidence and timing. Ask one clarifying question where a reply is authorised; accept a no. Closing means agreeing the next step the other person actually wants.

## Apply it by brand
**TA GuruLabs:** Show a real workflow or clearly marked synthetic scenario. Ask: “What did you do the last time this happened?” Internally classify feedback, repeat problem, evidence request or explicit commercial request. PAYE is a research-stage synthetic storyboard/case-lab, commercial gate closed: no outbound, price, pilot, production claims or HMRC submission/integration claims. Keep professional judgement human. Other products retain their own release gates. For ready products only, a verified public checkout can be the single next step.

**JingWu Media:** Let an original clip, comic excerpt or track earn attention. Distinguish audience reaction from a commission or licensing enquiry. For an authorised response to an inbound brief, capture asset, intended use, medium, territory, term and exclusivity before quoting; confirm rights and scope. Do not imply a release, availability, customer or permission without evidence. Sicario remains a work in progress; keep the teaser on hold until the public overview works and describes the stage accurately. Test one feedback prompt when cleared: “What would you want to see next: Mateo’s first scene or the world he’s trying to leave?”
