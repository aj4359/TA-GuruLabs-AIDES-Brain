# TA GuruLabs Digital Shop Window — Commercial Content Operating System
Version 1.0 | Internal playbook | 10 November 2026

## Objective
Turn LinkedIn, X, TikTok and other authorised channels into an elegant, low-pressure **B2B shop window**. Sell off-the-shelf business tools through verified self-service product links; route complex needs to a scoped custom-solution enquiry. Focus on recruiters, agency owners and specialist SMEs. Revenue first, without sacrificing truth, product quality, privacy or intellectual property.

## Positioning
TA GuruLabs — independent product studio.
We build practical tools that help businesses win better business, protect margins and operate more effectively. International outlook; do not position as local-only or claim unverified customers/offices.

## Editorial pillars
1. **See it work** (50%): 15–45-second mobile-readable screen demos of one practical job; original/licensed footage, readable captions, concrete buyer pain, single public product link.
2. **Position yourself to win** (30%): original business education, pricing, margins, cash conversion, opportunity qualification, negotiation, recruiting economics, productivity. Tie each lesson to a real decision the reader can make; no generic motivational filler.
3. **Inside the workshop** (20%): short build-in-public good/bad/indifferent lessons; show progress and genuine constraints without revealing prompts, source code, algorithms, client data or private architecture.

## Formats and production
- Primary: 20–45s original product clips, voiceover, captions, 9:16 social version; keep 16:9 master when relevant.
- Secondary: 2–5-minute AI-narrated audio explainers/podcast episodes with source notes and clear identification of synthetic narration when appropriate.
- Supporting: concise LinkedIn articles/carousels, X posts and useful one-page buyer guides.
- Content unit: buyer pain → observable example → financial/business implication with labelled assumptions → one low-pressure action.
- AURA routes production; Scout researches and source-checks; AUREUS Finance validates economics; Sales Genius writes buyer-focused hooks; Maestro handles original/licensed audio; CUTLASS handles original/licensed video, pacing and captions; Public Learning Desk owns editorial/publishing; Compliance checks factual claims, licensing, disclosure and permissions.
- Automate research triage, outline, scripting, asset assembly, format adaptation, QA checklist, scheduling and receipt capture using existing tools. Keep human approval gates for new spending, contracts and consequential claims. No new scheduled tasks.

## Publishing hard gates
1. Actual working product/demo or relevant public overview URL opens without owner-only access.
2. Strong original/licensed image, carousel or working video is attached and survives upload; no text-only posts.
3. Correct product stage and claims, mobile readability, captions/alt text, © TA GuruLabs.
4. Authorised connected account and publication permission, no duplication of already publishing posts.
5. Actual published URL/receipt captured; scheduled is not published. If any gate fails, hold only that post and repair the missing component.
6. Never claim revenue, customers, profitability outcomes or payment availability without evidence.
7. Never publish proprietary methods, internal agent prompts, private source media or confidential commercial strategy.

## Self-service commerce
Product page: buyer problem, 20–45s demo, 3 clear outcomes, exact deliverables, transparent price, purchase button to an individually verified Payhip checkout, setup requirements, support terms and optional 'Need something tailored?' enquiry.
- Start with one narrow HUNTR-X recruitment margin/opportunity tool; verify formulas with synthetic cases and iPhone usability.
- Optional premium custom engagement via brief enquiry form and scoped quotation, not a vague sales meeting.
- Contact email must be functionally tested; an email address is not a demo link.
- No external payment/checkout claim until independently tested. Avoid new subscriptions or paid ads.

## Reusing historical successful material
- Extract **ideas and principles**, not copyrighted phrasing, distinctive scripts, proprietary graphics, music or footage.
- Research primary sources, public-domain material or explicitly licensed works; track title, author, source, date, licence and transformation notes.
- Develop new examples for present-day SMEs and recruitment. Attribute distinctive ideas and direct quotations; don't imply endorsement by historic figures.
- Avoid close paraphrases of protected articles and do not train/copy someone else's proprietary paid course without permission.

## First campaign — recruitment economics
Series title: 'The Business Behind the Placement'
1. 'A vacancy isn't automatically profitable': screen-demo of editable permanent-placement fee and cost assumptions.
2. 'The rate looks good. Does the margin?': contractor bill/pay/on-cost scenario with clear assumptions.
3. 'Winning the wrong vacancy costs more than losing it': opportunity prioritisation using fictional data.
4. 'Why payment terms matter': illustrative cash timing and payroll exposure.
5. 'Build a better quote before you send it': quote-to-profit decision checklist.
All must link to a verified working HUNTR-X Profit Desk demo; until then, keep in editorial queue. No fabricated testimonials or financial outcomes.

## Scoreboard
Count only verified public posts, unique working demo visits where analytics exists, genuine inbound enquiries, completed checkouts, cash received, delivery costs and qualified custom scopes. Unknown != zero. Weekly learning: which buyer pain and demo drove actual interest? Prioritise first real revenue.

## Ownership
Public Learning Desk owns publication; Staged Release Review audits, does not duplicate campaigns. Publishing console target: in-house LinkedIn/X drafts, editorial approval, scheduling, receipts and analytics; do not depend on a paid Metricool plan. Founder has standing publication authority once all gates pass.
