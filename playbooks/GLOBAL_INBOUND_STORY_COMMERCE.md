# Global Inbound — The Art of Selling Without Selling
Version 1.0 | 10 November 2026 | TA GuruLabs internal commercial doctrine

## Thesis
Open the door globally through original, useful business storytelling. Stories are the invitation; practical tools are the proof; self-service checkout is the transaction; a tailored enquiry is the optional higher-value path. Never mislead or conceal a material product limitation. Do not solicit strangers or buy advertising.

## Audience
Start with recruitment agency owners and directors, including cold-desk builders, boutique search firms, temp/contract suppliers and MSP-facing agencies. Then adjacent SMEs with expensive commercial bottlenecks. Global availability does not imply all country-specific calculations are accurate; use generic configurable scenarios until local rules are checked.

## Story-led discovery funnel
1. **Hook:** 'You inherited a profitable desk. Could you build one from zero?' or 'Your agency is growing. Can it fund next Friday's payroll?'
2. **Insight:** one original, useful commercial principle, sourced where factual.
3. **Proof:** original 20–45s mobile-readable demonstration of one observable workflow.
4. **Invitation:** verified demo/product page, transparent scope and price; optional self-service purchase or custom-solution enquiry.
5. **Learning:** measure real inbound actions, not vanity impressions or assumed sales.

## Global readiness without high overhead
- Plain English international positioning, neutral currency input, localised examples and subtitles where viable.
- Pricing and checkout currency, taxes/VAT, digital consumer/business rules, refunds, export/privacy and payment-provider supported countries checked before enabling purchase in each market.
- Product claims must be geographically scoped; payroll, employment, tax and contractor on-cost rules are not universal.
- Support by asynchronous FAQ/help first; paid scoped human custom work only when viable.
- Maintain a single canonical product page with country-aware examples; no speculative offices, staff, customers or sales.
- Use existing no-cost tools, no new recurring tasks.

## IP, copyright and truth
Study public-domain or licensed historical business material for underlying ideas, then write original analysis and fresh recruitment examples. Credit source ideas where appropriate. Never copy distinctive prose, paid-course content, video, music or artwork. Share observed product behaviour and buyer value; keep algorithms, agent prompts, code, data, customer information and unpublished roadmaps internal. Do not lead every post with 'unproven'; do not imply a demo is a deployed commercial service.

## Release and commerce gates
Every external post needs original/licensed media + tested publicly accessible demo or overview + captions/alt text + © TA GuruLabs + accurate stage + authorised account + live publication receipt. Public Learning Desk owns posting, not staged review. Test product and mobile usability, contact route, individual Payhip checkout and delivery before saying 'buy now'. Do not create duplicates or claim scheduled posts are published.

## Initial measurable experiment
One HUNTR-X recruitment margin demo with editable synthetic scenarios; one short original video; one public page; one working enquiry path; one individually tested checkout if selling. Publish a story-led post after the gates pass. Track visits, completed demos, enquiries, purchases and cash received; unknown metrics remain unknown.
