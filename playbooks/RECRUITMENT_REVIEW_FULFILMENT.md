# Recruitment Readiness Review — fulfilment rehearsal

Internal only. The £149 draft service is not open for orders. No live buyer or purchase is involved here.

## Buyer intake
Request one vacancy advert, role requirements, selection stages and three business goals. Ask for role information only; exclude CVs and candidate/employee personal data. Confirm receipt and completeness before starting the five-working-day delivery clock. Confirm actual reviewer capacity, customer terms and seller setup before accepting payment.

## Fictional sample: maintenance scheduler
**Diagnostic:** “Flexible administrator” hides the work location and responsibility for engineer scheduling. Make location and hours explicit, describe the decision the employee owns and use the same role-related interview prompts for every applicant.

**Rewritten opening:** Maintenance Scheduler | On-site | Mon–Fri, 8 am–4 pm. Keep the engineer diary up to date, flag urgent repairs and tell customers when appointments change. Give one example of balancing competing appointments and keeping the people affected informed.

**Interview prompts:** (1) An urgent repair arrives when the only engineer is booked. What do you check and whom do you tell? (2) Two appointments appear under the same slot. How would you establish which booking is correct and record the fix? Score each 0 (no usable approach), 1 (partially checks or communicates), 2 (verifies facts, explains the trade-off, communicates and records next action). Use the rubric as a conversation aid; the employer decides.

**30-day plan:** Days 1–3 rewrite the advert and confirm hours and essential criteria; days 4–10 calibrate two interviewers using fictional answers; days 11–30 note where candidates need clarification and revise the advert based on recurring confusion.

## Handover and checkout evidence
1. Assemble diagnostic, advert, interview questions/rubric and 30-day plan into PDF or Doc. Human reviewer checks completeness and role facts; send to the verified buyer route. Include one advert revision and one written clarification under the launch kit.
2. Rehearse with the fictional vacancy. Record elapsed effort and who can cover the promised delivery window. Verify paid listing scope, terms, seller identity, payment readiness and destination in the seller account. Do not assume checkout access from a storefront or search result.
3. If supported for the selected product type, rehearse checkout and acknowledgement without payment. Independently confirm payment processor readiness; a zero-cost receipt is not a sale. Never buy from ourselves to claim revenue.
4. Once cleared, show the before/after in original media. One next step: verified checkout. Record a real external order ID, gross, fees and net (no buyer details in Git). No ad spend until the path passes.
