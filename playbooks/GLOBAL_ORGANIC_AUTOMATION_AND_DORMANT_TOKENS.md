# TA GuruLabs: global organic operating model (12 November 2026)

Status: OPERATING SPECIFICATION, not proof of deployed automation or revenue. Owner: founder; coordination: Public Learning Desk (publication), Scout (research), Night Brief (workflow evidence), Continuity Guard (creative and factual checks). No new ChatGPT scheduled tasks or paid services.

## Founder availability
Weekdays: AIDES perform routine preparation, preflight, permitted publication, feedback triage and receipts. Escalate only meaningful exceptions. Evenings/weekends: founder reviews material creative direction, legitimate collaborations, financial decisions and blockers. Never imply unattended execution unless a workflow run confirms it.

## Organic discovery and localisation
- Prioritise authorised TikTok and YouTube connections; LinkedIn, X and Instagram require independently confirmed permissions. Avoid paid engagement, giveaways, bought followers, fake accounts, engagement pods and unsolicited DMs.
- Create a distinct 8–20-second story-led hook from *approved* footage; no repeated identical clips or unsupported product claims. Include original/licensed media, captions, mobile-readable text, © TA GuruLabs and verified public destination.
- Prepare localisation variants based on genuine audience signals: English baseline; Arabic for Habibi, Punjabi for Turn Out the Light, then other languages only when relevant. Translate captions, descriptions, on-screen text and CTAs; preserve original song and cultural context. Human/native-language quality check for ambiguous idioms and sensitive claims. Do not auto-dub a singer or alter licensed audio without rights and approval.
- Publish only via confirmed authorised connections after media, rights, product/demo link and access checks. Public Learning Desk owns posts; Scout does not duplicate campaigns. Record platform URLs and actual status (draft/scheduled/live/failed), not assumed success.
- Use aggregate views, retention, shares, comments and verified demo visits to decide next formats; unsupported analytics are 'unavailable', not zero. Respond helpfully to legitimate inbound comments where account rights permit; no automatic group posting.

## Commercial path
Every product must have: working end-to-end mobile demo, clear value proposition, truthful offer, functional enquiry route, individual verified checkout if claiming for sale, and tested fulfilment. HUNTR-X Profit Desk is first test candidate. PAYE Forensics Pro commercial gate remains closed. No customer, sales or profit claims without receipts.

## Dormant-token opportunity desk: research only
Scope: publicly discoverable abandoned/dormant projects, tokens, treasury programmes and holder communities. Legitimate routes: voluntary token donations to a newly created studio-controlled wallet, openly negotiated purchases from lawful owners, authorised recovery of studio-owned assets, and paid software services for asset inventory/migration or community project revival. Dormant supply is not automatically available or owned by TA GuruLabs. Never seek seed phrases, abandoned wallet access, private keys, account takeovers or unclaimed funds belonging to others.

Each candidate requires: chain and verified contract; liquidity and market depth; ownership/permissions; token holder concentration; transfer restrictions and taxes; sanctions/UK eligibility; IP/trademark rights; community consent; exchange delisting and legal risks; technical security; clear commercial model. Flag honeypots, wash trading, fake volume, upgradeable contracts, impersonation and rug-pull patterns. Never value dormant tokens at historical peak prices or count donations as revenue. No trading, spending, wallet signatures or token acceptance without explicit founder approval and independent wallet security checks. No guaranteed returns, artificial volume, pump campaigns or price manipulation.

Deliverables: candidate research ledger with sources and confidence, an ethical donation/asset-intake policy draft, and a separate commercial service hypothesis. No live fundraising or wallet address until legal, security and approval gates pass.

## Weekly control
Daily existing release report: real publications, blocked media, verified leads, payment receipts and exceptions only. Weekend review: localisation performance, creative continuity, legitimate collaboration requests, candidate token due diligence and founder decisions. Prefer event-triggered workflows and short state handoffs to repeated polling.
