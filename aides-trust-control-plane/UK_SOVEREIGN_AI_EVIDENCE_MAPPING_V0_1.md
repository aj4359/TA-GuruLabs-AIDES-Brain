# UK Sovereign AI — evidence mapping v0.1

This is a readiness map, not a claim of eligibility, award, certification or compliance.

## Current programme facts to design against
The UK Sovereign AI R&D Procurement Scheme is aimed at UK-registered startups/SMEs developing demonstrator-stage capabilities. Published programme material states that the scheme removes some traditional turnover/cash-reserve barriers, allows companies to retain developed IP, and may support upfront payment where cash flow would otherwise prevent participation.

The first challenge set includes safe AI-agent adoption. Published material asks for evidence-based, operational and ideally automated approaches to agent risk management, including resilience and cyber-security risks, architecture, monitoring and controls. Open-source-compatible approaches are explicitly welcomed.

## Gap register before any application
- independent security review evidence
- production deployment architecture
- data protection impact assessment where personal data is involved
- accessibility evidence for user-facing surfaces
- incident response/support ownership
- recovery drill against a realistic deployment
- demonstrator case study with externally understandable metrics

## Positioning
Do not pitch a generic multi-agent platform. Position a bounded demonstrator:

**Governed autonomous work: constrain the mission, observe the work, recover from failure, and produce evidence for the decision-maker.**

No proprietary orchestration implementation needs to be disclosed in the buyer-facing pack.
