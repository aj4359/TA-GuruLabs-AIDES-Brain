# AIDES — 60-second proof demonstrator

## The customer story

**0–10s — Outcome**
A customer gives AIDES a bounded outcome, an authority reference and a maximum economic envelope.

**10–20s — Workforce**
AIDES considers available capabilities. Unapproved workers are rejected. Of the approved workers capable of producing the required evidence, AIDES selects the lower-cost eligible option.

**20–30s — Work + evidence**
The replaceable execution runtime performs the work. AIDES rejects a result that exceeds the authorised cost or arrives without evidence.

**30–40s — Recovery**
The action is reversible and carries a named recovery method, authority and checkpoint. Recovery is part of the contract rather than an afterthought.

**40–50s — Verification**
The result is not labelled verified merely because a worker says it succeeded. TA VERIFIED requires PASS + evidence + an explicit release decision.

**50–60s — Value**
The customer sees attributable cost and Cost per Verified Outcome.

## KISS buyer line

**Tell us the outcome. We constrain the workforce, record the work, prove the result and show what it cost.**

## What this demo does not claim
This fixture proves the control/evidence/economics chain in the repository. It is not evidence of a specific external runtime, government accreditation, production security certification or a customer deployment.
