# AIDES Mission Control — Proof v1

## What a reviewer should understand in 90 seconds
TA GuruLabs does not merely run autonomous workers. It records who commissioned work, which worker/configuration was eligible, how work was organised, what limits applied, whether a human gate was required, what the work cost, what outcome was observed and whether any resulting learning became authoritative.

## Public-safe demonstration mission
**Mission:** Identify one material market change relevant to TA GuruLabs and prepare an evidence-backed recommendation.

The demonstration is intentionally sanitised. It must never display private prompts, source-selection heuristics, customer data, credentials, private memory contents, proprietary routing logic or production topology.

## Visible proof chain
1. **Commission** — mission ID and commissioning authority reference.
2. **Allocate** — selected eligible worker/configuration and allocation evidence reference.
3. **Plan** — execution graph reference and hard envelope.
4. **Parallelise** — bounded research jobs with real dependencies only.
5. **Verify** — fresh-context correctness/current/source-real checks.
6. **Govern** — required authority and human gate state.
7. **Execute** — execution receipt or explicit blocked/not-executed state.
8. **Measure** — externally measured cost; unknown cost stays unknown.
9. **Outcome** — observed outcome and evidence reference; no automatic causation claim.
10. **Learn** — proposed/approved/rejected/superseded/revoked learning provenance.

## Reviewer rules
- Green/completed means the recorded stage completed. It does **not** prove business causation.
- `UNKNOWN` / `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` must remain visible rather than being converted into a positive claim.
- Verified economic value appears only after separate verification.
- Consequential action cannot appear as executed if a required human gate was not approved.
- Learning cannot appear authoritative merely because an AIDE proposed it.

## Commercial narrative
**Give TA GuruLabs a mission. Watch the operating system decide who is eligible to perform it, organise the work, control the envelope, verify the evidence, stop at consequential decisions, measure what happened and preserve only approved learning.**

This is a proof surface for governed digital work, not a claim that every underlying operation is autonomous or production-ready.
