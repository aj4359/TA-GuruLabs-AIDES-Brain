# AIDES Procurement Evidence Pack v0.1

Purpose: make governed autonomous work assessable by institutional/public-sector buyers without exposing proprietary implementation.

## Evidence gates
1. Product outcome — defined service outcome and acceptance criteria.
2. Authority — who/what may act, permission boundaries, high-risk approval.
3. Security — secrets handling, least privilege, dependency/supply-chain controls.
4. Data handling — data classes, retention, deletion, residency/provider assumptions.
5. Auditability — action, actor/worker, authority, evidence, timestamp, version.
6. Reversibility — checkpoint/recovery path or explicit irreversible-action warning.
7. Accessibility — user-facing surfaces assessed against applicable accessibility requirements.
8. Service/support — incident route, ownership, recovery and customer communication.
9. Economics — attributable cost and Cost per Verified Outcome where applicable.
10. Verification — TA VERIFIED record with limitations and release decision.

## Procurement posture
TA GuruLabs should not claim certification or compliance that has not been independently established. This pack is an evidence index: each claim must link to an artifact, test, policy or external assurance.

## KISS buyer narrative
**Tell us the outcome. We constrain the workforce, record the work, prove the result and show what it cost.**
