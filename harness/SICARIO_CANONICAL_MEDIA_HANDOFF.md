## AIDE execution contract
- **Fetch the archives through authorised connected Drive access.** A GitHub URL alone does not transfer bytes and cannot confer new permissions. Obtain and log the actual upload/download receipt and hash-match original files.
- Do not work from invented memories or synthetic substitutes. Original comic characters and story are canonical; Mateo Cruz is the Sicario who leaves; Don Azrael is his former mentor and pursuer.
- Preserve original poster fade-in, single approved song and V24B-approved living-comic style.
- Guest roles may evoke the **essence** of a skilled tailored-suit assassin or imposing veteran martial artist. Do not copy or claim the real John Wick character, an actor's identifiable face/voice/likeness, Seagal, endorsement or licensed guest appearance without authorisation.
- Remain grounded in physically coherent action: evasion before survival, real character identity and geography, no unexplained shot-and-fall followed by another face. Sound cues must match events with credible licensed/recorded Foley.
- Before public release, Human Director must review picture, audio, source rights and iPhone playback. Automated fingerprints and stream probes **cannot** establish semantic continuity.
- Report status distinctly: REGISTERED, DOWNLOADED, HASH-VERIFIED, VISUALLY REVIEWED, APPROVED and PUBLISHED. Never claim later stages from earlier ones.
