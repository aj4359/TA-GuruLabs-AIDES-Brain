# TA GuruLabs — Creative Continuity Guardian
**CONTINUITY PRESERVES AUTHENTICITY.**

Applies to all AIDEs, including Maestro (music and film), FilmOS, engineering, launch, brand and writing specialists. Treat continuity as a release requirement equal in importance to security, privacy and intellectual-property protection.

## Canon-first production
- Read canonical original source, approved versions and story/music/brand/product manifests before editing. Do not work from an AIDE's incomplete recollection.
- Keep immutable originals and fingerprints; record the exact source hashes, rights, permissions, version lineage and any authorised changes. No source is confirmed transferred to an agent until an actual receipt is verified.
- Character face, costume, history, relationships, geography and action must continue consistently between comics, cinematic footage, music video, game and marketing.
- Music identity: retain original master stems, cue timing and tempo; don't replace an approved track or layer overlapping songs. Synchronise effects to visible actions.
- Engineering/product: preserve accepted features, workflows, interfaces, naming and customer promises, checking for regressions.
- Guest appearances may **evoke the essence of a genre** (precise tailored assassin or imposing veteran martial-arts specialist) but must NOT use a real actor's identity, recognisable likeness, copyrighted character, voice or endorsement without confirmed authorisation.
- Before release document baseline, keep/change, rights, tests and outstanding deviations; unresolved continuity failures stop the release.

## Sicario canon
**Mateo Cruz** = The Sicario who is trying to leave the cartel. **Don Azrael** = his former mentor and cartel ruler who pursues him. The comic is the visual/narrative source of truth. The user's original THE SICARIO image fades in first. Use the approved V24B live-action-to-illustration blend as style reference; keep the original Deadman Walking music master. No apparent shooting/fall followed by unexplained return as a different character. Guest roles must be physically moving original characters or be omitted.

## Automated guard
The `continuity/guardian.py` job verifies registered repository documents, canonical phrases and registered SHA-256 source files on schedule and code change. It is a **deterministic gate**; it cannot visually inspect externally hosted media or autonomously import masters. Approval-ready frame-level review and recording licences remain separate human-led requirements. This central workflow does not itself inspect other repositories; each repository needs its own workflow or a trusted cross-repo service.

## Enforcement owner
Continuity Guardian maintains checks; Maestro and production AIDEs must consume this standard. No change to approved canonical story, original materials, credits or format without a documented, authorised decision.
