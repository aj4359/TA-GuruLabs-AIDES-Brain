# FilmOS shot-first production benchmark

Scope: JingWu Media and TA GuruLabs narrative video. Companion to CINEMATIC_SOUND_STANDARD.md and CREATIVE_CONTINUITY_GUARD.md.
Sources: Manus [video workflow](https://manus.im/features/video), [timeline](https://manus.im/features/video-editor), [model catalogue](https://manus.im/models), [reference inputs](https://manus.im/models/seedance-2-0) and [music-video tool limits](https://manus.im/tools/ai-music-video-generator). These document capabilities, not private implementation or the settings used in Anthony's renders.

## Production handoff
1. Cutlass identifies the approved edit and records keep/change, harm to continuity, and exact start/end timecodes. Freeze source footage, music and accepted edits; record hashes when actual files are available.
2. Storyboard the missing shot with entry frame, intended exit frame, actor action, background action, camera, duration and continuity references. Treat pans over a still as a distinct motion-comic technique, never as a replacement for a requested performance.
3. Route shots by capability: reuse approved real footage first; otherwise choose an authorised motion generator with reference controls. Keep provider and version, inputs, settings, attempt count and actual cost. Do not buy credits without a separate authorised budget. If no generator satisfies the shot, report the gap rather than rendering a fake substitute.
4. Maestro builds an audio cue sheet from the approved master. Edit to the actual track by timecode, mute generated music by default and layer only approved effects separately. AI-generated sound that matches a shot is not evidence of sync to the original song.
5. Assemble an editable timeline with separate picture, titles, original music and approved effects. Swap rejected shots only. Export a short review segment before touching the master; deliver a mobile playback check and separate technical decode result.

## First benchmark: Turn On The Light
Keep v23 untouched. Pick the first agreed weak transition into the dance once the source is inspected; isolate 10–15 seconds and record actual timecodes. Keep the approved intro, line dancers, track, founder likeness and band ending. If the original clip and a comparable Manus output exist, compare those; otherwise use the same authorised source frames, song excerpt, brief and aspect ratio for one Manus and one internal/provider-neutral attempt, with no additional spend assumed. If only one route is available, label it a baseline review, not an A/B result.

Measure usable seconds, elapsed time, rerenders and real cost. Review both clips at matched loudness for subject motion, character/cast match, continuity into and out of the shot, edit alignment to marked beats, sound artefacts and iPhone viewing. Pass requires visible intended subject action, all protected elements still present in context, no doubled soundtrack, and reviewer-confirmed beat alignment. Record preferred take and why; don't extrapolate one shot to whole film quality. Do not replace or hold the whole release because a benchmark is incomplete. No footage is claimed generated by this protocol.
