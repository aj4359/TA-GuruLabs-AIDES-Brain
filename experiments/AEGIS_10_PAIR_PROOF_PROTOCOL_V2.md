# AEGIS — 10-Pair Proof Protocol v2

Status: proof before scale
Date: 2026-10-01

## Hypothesis
A deliberate, paced introduction with AEGIS's BEFORE + Sealed Reveal journey can produce stronger conversation quality and mutual willingness to continue than a catalogue/swipe-style interaction.

## Experimental unit
10 consenting pairs. One deliberate introduction at a time.

## Preserve differentiation
The experiment must test the AEGIS journey, not merely a matching algorithm:
- BEFORE
- guided values / habits / preferences / deal-breakers
- witnessed identity / trust signals where appropriate
- Sealed Reveal / progressive revelation
- prompt-led conversation support
- mutual consent before increased disclosure

## Measures
For each pair capture:
- introduction accepted / declined
- first meaningful response
- reciprocal response
- conversation depth/completion
- mutual willingness to continue
- reasons for stopping, where voluntarily supplied
- safety/reporting events
- qualitative feedback on pace, trust and relevance

Do not claim improved matching from 10 pairs. Treat results as directional product evidence.

## Gate
No public-scale launch decision until the experiment is complete, evidence is reviewed, safety/privacy issues are resolved and the result is documented.

## Competitive lesson
“One match at a time” is not the moat. The defensible layer is the experience and evidence created by BEFORE, progressive revelation, trust, conversation quality and the relationship journey.
