# HUNTR-X — Recruiter MVP PRD v0.1
Status: draft for validation, not built or launched. Owner: TA GuruLabs.
## Purpose
A browser-first specialist recruiter workspace that qualifies BOTH sides of a search before effort is invested. Recruiter product first; jobseeker product remains distinct.
## Target customer and wedge
Independent specialist recruiters and small agencies delivering difficult-to-fill roles, starting with multilingual technology/product/sales hires and one selected pilot specialty. Confirm this wedge through interviews; do not assert proven demand.
## North-star outcome
A recruiter can create a structured, transparent, mutually committed search brief and identify blockers in under 15 minutes (target to test, not a fact).
## Minimum useful workflow
1. Create client mandate: real hiring pain, deadline, decision-maker access, interview process, fee agreement, exclusivity/retainer status.
2. Capture job reality: base salary and currency, bonus/commission and uncertainty, hours, work location and remote policy, travel, holiday, benefits, reporting line, team, training, dress expectations, opportunities and downsides. Mark unknown items rather than invent.
3. Qualify candidate: specialist skills/evidence, languages and proficiency, work authorisation by jurisdiction, availability, compensation expectations, location/relocation, family or personal constraints only when volunteered and relevant, counteroffer stance and explicit consent to representation.
4. Score both sides transparently with a configurable readiness rubric. Seed 72/100 is a **pilot threshold**, not validated science; visible breakdown and manual override rationale. Do not automate exclusion from employment.
5. Produce two permission-aware packs: candidate-facing honest opportunity brief and client-facing consented candidate representation. No CV sharing without consent.
6. Track commitments, actions, interview stages, feedback deadlines and search status.
7. Generate a proposed fee/engagement summary for human approval before sending.
## Excluded from v0.1
Auto-submitting candidates, covert AI impersonation, automatic hiring/rejection, scraping private profiles, reference-to-sales repurposing, payments integration before provider approval, multi-sector marketplace, unverified candidate claims.
## First three screens
A. Mandate intake (KISS guided questions and missing-info flags).
B. Candidate intake and consent (role-specific fit and counteroffer readiness).
C. Mutual commitment view (side-by-side score reasons, gaps, next actions; exportable briefs).
## Acceptance tests
- Missing salary, travel, remote or job downsides show “not supplied”, never fake information.
- No candidate details can be shared without explicit tracked consent.
- No automated adverse hiring decision is made from a score.
- Both readiness scores trace back to individual answers and can be corrected.
- No prospecting use of referee information without separate lawful basis, notice and necessary permission.
- Sensitive personal data does not appear in application logs or publicly accessible repos.
- A complete demo runs on fabricated data and passes keyboard/mobile checks.
## Commercial experiment (first 30 days)
Week 1: conduct 5 structured discovery interviews with specialist recruiters and 3 hiring managers; track pain, willingness-to-pay, alternatives.
Week 2: clickable browser prototype with fictional records, demo two-sided qualification.
Week 3: test with 3 willing pilot recruiters; record completion time and error rate.
Week 4: offer paid pilot only after actual value is demonstrated, terms reviewed and working payment/contact route verified.
Metrics: interview evidence, minutes per brief, required-field completeness, consent compliance, paid pilot conversations and actual revenue (£0 until independently evidenced).
## Delivery discipline
Idea → PRD → engineering task → branch/PR → tests → staging → pilot → approved launch.
Do not market this as deployed, live or compliant-by-certification without evidence.
