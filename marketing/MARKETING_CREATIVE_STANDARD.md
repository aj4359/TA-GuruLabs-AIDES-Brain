# TA GuruLabs — Marketing Creative Standard (2026-11-09)

Owner: TA GuruLabs | Applies to all marketing/publishing AIDEs, including SIGMA and Jingwu Media.

## Principle
**Publish like a premium independent studio, not an automated content farm.** Each public item must have a clear audience, a single compelling idea, human/customer benefit, distinctive visual or video asset, evidence/claim check, and a purposeful next step. Build in public without pretending that research is a launched product.

## Brand architecture
- TA GuruLabs: umbrella independent product and creative studio.
- Jingwu Media: media/music/film imprint within TA GuruLabs, not the entire studio.
- SIGMA: TA GuruLabs experimental research programme for autonomous crypto market intelligence/trading; not a proven live profit-making system.
- Avoid inventing registered corporate/legal structures: describe brands and imprints accurately.
- Avoid unverified founder, customer, profit, or operational claims.

## Publication gate: all must pass
1. **Angle:** Who is this for? What pain, opportunity, curiosity, or emotion does it address?
2. **Hook:** First line/frame conveys a specific reason to care; no filler, empty hype or cliched "revolutionary/game changing" slogans.
3. **Story:** Problem → specific insight or demonstrable work → benefit or question → one CTA.
4. **Proof:** Link to working public demo where available, with verified access and truthful stage label: concept/research/prototype/validated/live. If no demo exists, state so; never create fake proof.
5. **Visual:** EVERY post/article has a copyright-cleared striking original image, demonstrative screenshot or polished video. Thumbnail/frame one must stop the scroll. Video must have subtitles/safe zones, cinematic audio mixing (where appropriate), and mobile playback QA. Use project canon and continuity checks; preserve original approved assets.
6. **Design:** Consistent TA GuruLabs identity: near-black/charcoal base, intentional contrast, restrained metallic/teal accent for tech, typographic hierarchy, generous whitespace, readable at 390px, discreet © TA GuruLabs/Jingwu Media as appropriate. Brand theme may vary for artistic media, with clear imprint attribution. No generic robot-head or fake-trading-gains stock art.
7. **Copy:** Crisp, confident British English; avoid overexplaining, AI-sounding repetition, hashtag stuffing, and multiple competing CTAs. Show what happened, what changed and why it matters.
8. **Risk:** Crypto content is educational/research, not financial advice or an invitation to invest; assess UK financial promotion risks before publishing. Obtain necessary permissions for likenesses and third-party media. No claims of guaranteed returns. Never publish secrets, private documents or unlicensed footage.
9. **Channel:** LinkedIn: evidence-rich founder/operator perspective. TikTok/Reels: immediate visual hook, subtitles, one story in 15–35s. X: concise insight plus asset. Adapt copy for platform; do not mechanically cross-post identical captions.
10. **Access/proof:** Verify artwork/video file exists, actual playback, public link, caption accuracy, CTA destination, and account destination BEFORE publishing. Record URL, timestamp, asset version and receipt after publication.
11. **Authority:** AIDEs may publish pre-approved product categories to connected LinkedIn accounts without per-post approval once ALL gates pass; TikTok and Instagram publishing is likewise permitted only where connected permissions allow and all gates pass. Never imply that accounts are connected or a post is live without verification. Escalate legal, reputational, financial promotion, or copyright concerns for human review.
12. **Metrics:** Review watch-through, saves, relevant comments, demo click-through, genuine inquiries and actual verified revenue; change messaging based on observations rather than vanity follower counts.

## Content series
- Built at the Kitchen Table — founder/build-in-public narrative.
- Proof, Not Promises — verified demonstrations and experiments.
- Behind the AIDEs — tangible workflow improvement.
- Jingwu Media Presents — music, film, comics, canonical trailers.
- What We Broke and Fixed — genuine engineering learning.

## AIDE roles
- Creative Director: angle, positioning and design system.
- Copy Chief: hooks, human-first messaging, platform variants.
- Visual Producer / Maestro: original visuals, sound and edit.
- Continuity/Compliance: verification, rights, claim review.
- Publisher: publish via connected authorized routes, retain receipts, track outcome.
No publish action may bypass the release gate for speed.

## Example SIGMA framing
Headline: "I spent years beside a trading floor. Now I'm building the system I once wanted to use."
Label: "SIGMA — Research in progress".
CTA: "Follow our public experiments." Only say continuous scans, trading performance or successful automation when documented by real run evidence.
