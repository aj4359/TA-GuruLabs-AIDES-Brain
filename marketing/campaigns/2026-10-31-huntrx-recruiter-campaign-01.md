## Proposed social copy
**Is that vacancy actually ready to recruit?**

A role arrives. The job title looks clear. But the decision-maker, timeline or interview process may still be missing.

And a candidate who looks promising on paper may not yet be ready to move.

At TA GuruLabs, we're exploring HUNTR-X Recruiter: a guided approach to checking the client brief, understanding candidate readiness and flagging unanswered questions for human judgement.

Our accompanying video is a **concept simulation using synthetic scenarios**, not evidence of a production deployment or verified customer outcomes.
