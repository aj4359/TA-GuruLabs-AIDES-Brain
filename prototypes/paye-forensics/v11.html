3 · Customer action layer
What we can safely say
If some allowance appears unused while another PAYE source is being taxed in full, the app can flag a possible allocation check. HMRC says unused allowance from a lower-paying main job may sometimes be allocated to another job depending on the circumstances, and customers can contact HMRC to check whether their allowance can be split.
What we must not say
“Change your code to X.” HMRC issues the code. The product should instead say: “Check your estimated incomes, check which job is using your Personal Allowance, and ask HMRC whether a split allocation is appropriate.”
